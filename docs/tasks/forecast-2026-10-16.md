# First forecasting runs (2026-10-16)

Forecasting mode (33f2b77): training draws a random cutoff per clip; frames
after it get no image information. Validation cuts at frame 12 of 24 and scores
seen frames (0-11) and future frames (12-23) separately. Leak check:
`scripts/check_forecast.py`. Scores below: val, 349 clips, 24 grid, step 3,000.

| Future frames | All points | Moving points |
|---|---|---|
| "Nothing moves" from frame 0 | 0.281 | 0.129 |
| Diffusion (run28_fc_fl24), one sample | 0.435 | 0.161 |
| Diffusion, best of 5 samples | 0.510 | 0.206 |
| No-noise (run28_fc_rg24) | 0.485 | 0.206 |
| Freeze the model's own frame-11 guess, no-noise | 0.454 | 0.197 |
| Freeze the model's own frame-11 guess, diffusion | 0.424 | 0.168 |
| Freeze the TRUE frame-11 position | 0.547 | 0.321 |
| Keep the TRUE last speed | 0.528 | 0.317 |

Findings:
- The future part adds almost nothing over freezing the model's own track
  (moving 0.206 against 0.197); diffusion is below its own freeze (0.161 against 0.168).
- The biggest loss is the tracked position at the cut: freezing the true frame-11
  position gives 0.321, freezing our own guess 0.197. Better tracking of the
  seen frames is the largest lever for forecasting as well.
- Diffusion's best of 5 equals no-noise's single guess on moving points.
- Known small leak: each clip's scale is measured from depth over all frames.
